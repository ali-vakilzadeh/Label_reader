  • Ship the hex table above as colors.xml with the token names as resource names. No Material dynamic colour, no tonal palettes: the cream and navy pairing is fixed.
  • Bundle Noto Sans and Noto Sans Mono as font resources. Do not fall back to Roboto — the dashboard and the paperwork are set in Noto.
  • Touch targets 48dp, above the 44px web floor. Density conversion at xhdpi: 1rem = 16dp, so card padding 16dp, screen padding 14dp, tab bar 56dp.
  • CameraX preview fills the viewport; the gold bracket overlay is a static view, not an animated reticle. No entrance animation, no shutter flash beyond a 120ms fill change.
  • Elevation: use a 1dp stroke plus a 1dp shadow. Never a shadow without a stroke.
  • Photo capture writes to app-private storage; the row stays in the local queue until the middleware acknowledges it. The status bar shows offline · queue n as info, never critical.
  • Manifest: display: standalone, background_color #fbf7ee, theme_color #0f2439, portrait-primary.
  • Camera through getUserMedia with facingMode: environment; fall back to <input type="file" capture> where it is blocked. State the reason in a warning notice when permission is refused.
  • Self-host the fonts. Nothing loads from a CDN in production; the office is often offline.
  • Service worker caches the shell and queues submissions in IndexedDB. The queue count in the status bar is the source of truth the operator trusts.
  • Safe areas: pad the brand bar with env(safe-area-inset-top) and the tab bar with env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • prefers-reduced-motion zeroes every duration. There are no spinners; a pending vision pass shows a meter with its own figure.
